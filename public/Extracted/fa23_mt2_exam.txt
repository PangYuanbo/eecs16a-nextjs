room.

computers, other electronic devices, or scratch paper are allowed.

should be adequate. If you still run out of space, please use a blank page and clearly tell us in the

Show all of your work in order to receive full credit. Partial credit will be given for substantial progress on
each problem. Do not forget units when relevant.

If you need to use the restroom during the exam, bring your student ID card, your phone, and your exam to
a proctor. You can collect them once you return from the restrooms.

Our advice to you: if you can't solve the problem, state and solve a simpler one that captures at least some
of its essence. You might get some partial credit, and more importantly, you will perhaps find yourself on a
path to the solution.

Good luck!
